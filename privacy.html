Privacy policy

PageWeave processes the webpage you choose to capture or record. Its extension and companion importer do not send captures, recordings, account details or analytics to OmniCan.ca.

What the extension accesses

After you invoke a feature, PageWeave reads the selected page’s visible layout, text, colours, images and supported vector content. It can take a screenshot to reproduce media. Recording accesses the video of that selected tab; it does not access the microphone, camera or audio.

Where content goes

Captures are downloaded as JSON with embedded assets where supported. Recordings are encoded locally and downloaded as WebM. Temporary video chunks are stored in browser-local origin-private storage when available, or memory otherwise. Temporary disk files are removed after completed or interrupted downloads. A crash or abrupt browser shutdown may leave temporary files; clearing the extension’s data or uninstalling it removes that browser-local data. Your downloaded files remain until you delete them.

Recreated images or media may load from their original website addresses. Figma imports are stored in the Figma file you choose and are subject to Figma’s own service and privacy practices. These are separate from PageWeave’s local processing.

Sharing and retention

OmniCan.ca does not receive, sell or use captured content for advertising, tracking or training. PageWeave has no account system or analytics. You control whether and where to share your exported files, which may include sensitive content from the selected page.

Permissions

activeTab and scripting allow the user-invoked page capture and animation. tabCapture records only the requested tab. offscreen keeps the local recorder alive after the popup closes. downloads saves requested WebM files and monitors PageWeave’s own downloads to clean up temporary video storage.

This website

This landing page has no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub hosts the public landing page and downloadable releases and may process ordinary request information under its own privacy policy.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Questions

Contact the developer through OmniCan.ca or PageWeave support. Avoid posting private captures or recordings in public support issues.

Three ways to keep a great page

01

From browser
to design file.

Export the visible viewport or loaded full page. Import the capture with the companion Figma development plugin to get real text, shapes, supported vectors and image layers.

A useful starting point for design studies. Complex CSS and fonts can need cleanup; components and Auto Layout aren’t inferred.

02

Watch the
pieces return.

A local swarm of animated cursors reconstructs the page’s appearance. Set the pace, take a scrolling Tour, replay it, or return to the live webpage underneath.

The swarm is an animation, with no AI service or multiplayer connection.

03

Small details.
Sharp recordings.

Record at the tab’s display-pixel resolution, with a target of 60 fps and a bitrate that scales with your screen. Save automatically after the rebuild or stop early.

Silent, compressed WebM. Frame rate depends on Chrome and your hardware. Keep the tab visible for best results.

Your page stays
in your workflow.

No PageWeave account. No analytics. No developer-operated upload service. Captures and video encoding run locally in your browser; requested files download to your computer.

Exported files contain page content. Original media can still load from its website, and designs imported into Figma use Figma’s own storage.
